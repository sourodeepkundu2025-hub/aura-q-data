# Community conduct

This project welcomes careful questions, reproducibility work and constructive criticism from researchers and developers of different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful and specific. Critique methods, evidence and code rather than people. Acknowledge others' work, distinguish observations from assumptions, and help contributors understand the reason for a requested change.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, repeated personal attacks and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Keep issues relevant to the project and do not post credentials, private provider records or confidential material.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed. Report a concern privately to Sourodeep Kundu at `sourodeep.kundu2025@vitstudent.ac.in`. Reports will be handled with discretion; no fixed response-time guarantee is made.
